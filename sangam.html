Sangam — संगम Retreat

Nonviolent Communication - A Language of Life

A Bilingual NVC Residential Retreat in Panchgani

Come home to deeper listening, honest expression, and compassionate connection — in the language of your heart.

Sangam is a residential retreat where participants practice Nonviolent Communication (NVC) in both Hindi and English — speaking, reflecting, and connecting in the language that feels most natural.

This retreat brings together learning and lived experience, making communication more natural, inclusive, and rooted in our everyday lives.

Register & Secure your Place

✨ Objectives — By the end of the retreat

Understand motivation

What inspires human behaviour beneath the surface

Deep listening & empathy

Listen beyond words and connect empathically

Honest expression

Speak your truth with care and clarity

Navigate conflict

Transform friction into understanding

Who can join? Sangam is for you if...

  • Learners, facilitators, educators & helping professionals
  • Parents, community builders & leaders
  • Anyone longing for more compassion, awareness and authentic connection
  • Those curious to explore NVC in a shared learning space (open to beginners & experienced practitioners)

✨ We invite you to participate with curiosity, openness and a willingness to practice.

Bilingual Support

Hindi | English | Accessible

Sangam takes this step to make NVC more accessible and rooted in the Indian context. Hindi-speaking assistants will provide live support. Worksheets and written material available in both Hindi and English.
